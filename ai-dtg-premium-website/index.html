INDUSTRY CHALLENGES

Critical Problems in Logistics

The logistics industry faces critical challenges that hinder carbon reduction and safety improvements

01

Data Collection Impossibility

Due to lease contract structures, logistics companies cannot collect truck data from owner-operators who refuse cooperation, making carbon emission calculation impossible

Impact: No carbon data = No emission reduction
02

Unpreventable Safety Accidents

Without real-time monitoring systems, driver fatigue and drowsiness cannot be detected, resulting in massive cargo damages and casualties every year

Impact: Annual billions in cargo damage
03

Unfair Carbon Allocation

Logistics companies allocate emissions by revenue ratio instead of using ISO-14083 standard, causing premium service providers to show inflated per-ton-km emissions, resulting in carbon loss

Impact: Unfair competition & carbon injustice
